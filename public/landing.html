// WHY HELLO CAT

          
THEORY IS NOT A SKILL.

          

            Hello Cat exists because reading about a vulnerability teaches you very little about
            exploiting it. Every exercise here is worked by hand, in a shell, against a lab that
            behaves like the real thing — and every target is fictional.
          

        


        

          

            
01

            
REAL SKILLS, NOT SLIDES

            

              Reconnaissance, web, cryptography, forensics, OSINT and scripting are solved with the
              same tools an analyst reaches for: enumeration, decoding, reading logs, writing a
              three-line script.
            

          


          

            
02

            
NOTHING REAL IS TOUCHED

            

              Every host, file and service in the range is fictional. The terminal runs against an
              in-memory filesystem: no host command executes and no packet leaves your machine, so
              the worst case is a ruined exercise.
            

          


          

            
03

            
ONE LOOP, END TO END

            

              Pick a target, read the objective, investigate, submit the flag, earn XP, unlock the
              next lab. No lecture series to finish first, no progress that lives in a spreadsheet.
            

          


          

            
04

            
PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE

            

              XP, eight ranks from Script Kiddie to Root Access, eight achievements, daily
              operations with streaks, and a leaderboard that resets every week.
            

          


          

            
05

            
PRACTICE THAT FITS

            

              One daily operation, four drills (quiz, command reconstruction, terminal puzzle,
              crypto) and priced or free hints when you are stuck. Ten minutes a day counts.
            

          


          

            
06

            
HELP IN THE CHANNEL

            

              Briefings, hints and reviews happen in Discord, next to the people working the same
              box. The bot answers with a route map instead of a wall of documentation.

// THE WEB TERMINAL

          
A KALI DESK, IN A BROWSER.

          

            The challenges are worked in a browser terminal that mimics a Kali workstation. It is
            fully simulated — an in-memory filesystem, a fixed command registry, no shell, no
            network calls to arbitrary hosts.

// FAQ

          
THE QUESTIONS THAT ALWAYS COME UP.

        


        

          

            Is this real hacking?
            

              It is real practice, on imaginary machines. Every target is a fictional lab scenario
              built for training. You are authorised — the systems are not real, and the terminal
              never touches your computer.
            

          

          

            Do I have to install anything?
            

              No. Discord plus a browser is the whole requirement set. The terminal runs entirely
              in the page, so a phone is enough to work a challenge on the go.
            

          

          

            Why is Discord the only platform?
            

              Because the community is the product. The bot, the briefings, the hints, the
              moderation and the leaderboard all live in one Discord server, and using the same
              identity everywhere keeps your progress and your account in sync. There is no second
              client to support and no second login to leak.
            

          

          

            I am a beginner. Where do I start?
            

              With the Easy tier. Start at Linux, read the objective, enumerate before you attack.
              If you get stuck, the hints are one command away, and the channel will walk you
              through a first target with you.
            

          

          

            Can I use my phone?
            

              Yes. The terminal is a web application, so it adapts to the screen: the keyboard
              shortcuts are optional and every command can be typed with the on-screen keyboard.
              The two call-to-action buttons stay within reach at the bottom of the screen.
            

          

          

            What are CATCOINS?
            

              Cosmetic points earned by capturing flags, finishing daily operations and playing the
              drills. They buy badges and titles. They have no real-world monetary value and cannot
              be bought, sold or cashed out.
            

          

          

            Is there an official website?
            

              Yes — this one, served from {{SITE_URL}}. Be careful with lookalike
              domains and with anyone who asks for your Discord password, your token or a payment.
              We never do.
